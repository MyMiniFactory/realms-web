The Feed

The Feed is your landing page, your at a glance summary of everything that is new. It shows posts from the people that you follow, their characters, their projects and any updates posted to campaigns that you are a part of.

Profiles

This is where you can build your own identity and your characters’ identities in Realms. Add information about yourself, upload a profile picture, a cover photo. Then start adding your characters. Each character gets their own profile associated with yours. Just as you can add your friends to your network, your characters can also add their friends in the form of other people’s characters. Share art of your character, diary entries or just tell us more about them in blog posts.

Have your characters take our personality quiz to learn more about who they are. You built your Paladin to be selfless and heroic - but is he really? Let our questions guide you through hypothetical scenarios and see what your character is really like. Your campaign mates can also share their thoughts about your characters, you might believe he is the hero, but do those who travel with him agree?

Campaign Dashboard

As a Dungeon Master, create a private space for you and your friends to talk about your campaign. Share artworks, secrets, favourite moments and memes. Create boards for different topics, collect all the artwork for your campaign in one place. Schedule sessions, giving your players and their characters a chance to RSVP. After each session, analyse your fellow characters, share what you think makes them tick, share what you viewed as their standout moments. Help your friends understand how their own characters are being perceived by others. By the end of the campaign, you will have collectively built a catalogue of your adventures for you to look back on.

Projects

Don’t just post separate images, post a journey. Projects is a way to connect a timeline, a thread, connecting individual posts relating to the same project. For example, miniature painting. Rather than having unconnected posts for ‘3D printed the model’, ‘primed it’, ‘base colours’, ‘highlights and shading’, ‘basing’ - tie them all together under one project. If you see a work in progress that you like, press a button to be shown the whole journey up until now in one place. Track your progress, meet your deadlines and let others witness your creative journey through Projects.
